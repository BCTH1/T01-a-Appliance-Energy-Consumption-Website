Australian Household Appliance Energy Overview

Energy rating labels help Australian households compare the running costs and energy efficiency of common domestic appliances.

National Energy Consumption Highlights

Standby power alone accounts for up to 10% of average household electricity usage across Australia. Investing in higher star-rated appliances significantly reduces lifetime operating costs and greenhouse gas emissions.

Television Energy Ratings

In the Australian market, television energy consumption varies widely based on panel display technology (OLED, QLED, LED) and screen size.
